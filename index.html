La Maison

A Love Letter to Lace

MARZÉ was born from a simple belief: every woman deserves a moment that feels like a painting. Inspired by the gilded salons of Versailles, the softness of ribbon and the quiet romance of antique lace, we built a wardrobe meant to be shared.

Our gowns are rented, not retired — worn to débuts, weddings and candlelit dinners, then lovingly restored for the next chapter. Luxury, without the closet full of once-worn dresses.

Questions

Petites Questions

How much is a rental, and for how long?

Every rental is ₱500 for three days. Collect the day before your event and return two days after. Longer rentals can be arranged on request.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend reserving two to four weeks before your event, especially during débutante and wedding season. Last-minute? Message us — we'll always try.

What if it doesn't fit?

That's what the fitting is for. We offer complimentary minor alterations, and if a gown simply isn't right, we'll help you choose another.

What happens if the dress gets damaged?

Small spills and loose threads are covered — that's part of our care. For significant damage, we'll assess it gently and fairly with you.

Do I need to clean it before returning?

Please don't! Professional cleaning is included. Simply return it in its garment bag and we'll take it from there.
